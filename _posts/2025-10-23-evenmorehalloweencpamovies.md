+# Thirteen Even MORE Terrible Halloween CPA Movies
+
+Halloween season still has a few days left, which means there is plenty of time for one last movie marathon full of spooky spreadsheets and terrifying tax forms. Dim the lights, grab your calculator, and prepare yourself for a baker's dozen of fresh financial frights.
+
+<B>1. The Silence of the IRS</B>
+
+A rookie special agent must outwit a cannibalistic auditor before the filing deadline, but the auditor is more interested in eating interest deductions.
+
+<BR>
+
+<B>2. Return of the W-2 Dead</B>
+
+Zombie payroll clerks claw their way out of the filing cabinets and issue duplicate wage statements to everyone in town.
+
+<BR>
+
+<B>3. The 1040 Exorcist</B>
+
+An experienced tax preparer is called in to banish a demonic entity that keeps claiming disallowed deductions.
+
+<BR>
+
+<B>4. It Follows (the Paper Trail)</B>
+
+Every time you sign a document, an otherworldly compliance officer appears to demand supporting documentation.
+
+<BR>
+
+<B>5. Close Encounters of the Third Party Verification</B>
+
+Aliens arrive, but they refuse to communicate unless you reconcile their confirmation letters.
+
+<BR>
+
+<B>6. The Spreadsheet on Elm Street</B>
+
+A nightmare creature haunts your formulas and introduces circular references you can never debug.
+
+<BR>
+
+<B>7. Poltergeist Depreciation Schedule</B>
+
+Possessed furniture keeps reclassifying itself from 7-year to 15-year property while whispering about bonus depreciation.
+
+<BR>
+
+<B>8. The Cabin in the GAAP</B>
+
+Five friends retreat to audit training, only to discover an ancient codification that demands ever more footnote disclosures.
+
+<BR>
+
+<B>9. Interview With the Vatable</B>
+
+An immortal vampire accountant cannot stop talking about value-added tax thresholds over a 200-year audit history.
+
+<BR>
+
+<B>10. Scary Carryback</B>
+
+An eerie voice keeps insisting you amend every return from the last five years to use up an NOL.
+
+<BR>
+
+<B>11. The Ledger Witch Project</B>
+
+Found footage reveals a group of interns lost in the woods searching for a missing trial balance.
+
+<BR>
+
+<B>12. The Haunting of Hill Schedule A</B>
+
+A mysterious mansion torments its new owners with itemized deductions that vanish the moment they look away.
+
+<BR>
+
+<B>13. Saw XBRL</B>
+
+You wake up chained to a desk with only a keyboard and an SEC filing template; the only way out is to tag every disclosure before sunrise.
+
